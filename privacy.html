Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Knuckle ("we", "us") handles information when you use our games, including Aeroshoot (the "Games"). By using the Games, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Our Games do not require you to create an account or provide your name or email to play. We and our partners may automatically collect:

  • Device and identifier data — device model, operating system version, language, and an advertising identifier (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
  • Usage data — in-game events, levels played, session length, and similar gameplay analytics.
  • Diagnostic data — crash logs and performance information used to fix bugs.

We do not knowingly collect your real name, phone number, or precise location.

2. How we use information

  • To run, maintain, and improve the Games.
  • To display advertising, including through third-party ad networks.
  • To analyze how the Games are used and to fix problems.

3. Advertising and third-party partners

Our Games show ads served through mediation. These partners may collect and process device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin (MAX mediation) — applovin.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network — facebook.com/about/privacy
  • Unity Ads — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Your choices

  • Personalized ads — you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • iOS tracking (ATT) — on iOS, you choose whether to allow tracking when the Game first asks.
  • EEA/UK and California — where applicable, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and to opt out of certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

5. Children's privacy

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Data retention and transfers

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Our partners may process data on servers located in other countries; their policies describe those transfers.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we will update the "Last updated" date above.
